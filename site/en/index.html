2Discord voice

Walk into a house,
your voice follows

In person, a private chat means slipping into the hallway while everyone watches. Here, whichever house you walk into on the map, Discord moves you into that house's voice channel.

  • Create

    The bot creates a voice channel for that house and moves you in. Only the people inside can see it.

  • Lock

    The people inside can lock the door. Anyone outside can only knock: let them in, or pretend nobody is home.

  • Clean up

    When everyone leaves, the channel is deleted. Ring the bell to gather and everyone goes back to the Clock Tower Square.

    3AI Storyteller

    Nobody wants to be
    the Storyteller? The AI Host will.

    The Storyteller has to know the rules, track everyone's character, invent false information and keep the game balanced: the hardest seat to fill. The AI Host does it for you. The Host can also run the game as a human Storyteller, reviewing and rewriting every piece of night information.

    1. 01

      Setup

      53 recommended setups, official ones first. If the player count doesn't fit one, the AI balances a setup itself: no lopsided games.

    2. 02

      Night

      At nightfall everyone is muted and deafened, then woken one by one in the official night order. As soon as everyone has chosen, the night resolves: no waiting around.

    3. 03

      False info

      Drunk and poisoned players get false information that stays consistent: they get a whole "fake world", and other players still have ways to disprove it.

    4. 04

      Balance

      Whichever side is behind gets a hand, within the rules: how many Minions are dead, how suspected the Demon is, how many good players have died.

    5. 05

      Journal

      Every ruling goes into the Storyteller's journal, revealed when the game ends. The Storyteller is not a black box.

    Storyteller's journalRevealed at the end · example

        FAQ

        Anything missing? Open an issue on GitHub.

        Ask a question
        Is the game in English?

        Yes. Open play.rambooo.com/?lang=en, or switch in the game under Settings → Language. Character names, abilities and script names use the official English. Every player picks their own language, so English and Chinese players can share a table; the narration voice follows each player's language too.

        What permissions does the bot need, and why?

        View channels, send messages, connect to voice, manage channels, manage roles, move members, mute members, deafen members. Muting and deafening is how everyone "closes their eyes" at night; moving members puts you into a house's voice channel; managing channels and roles sets permissions on the private-chat rooms, so a locked door really keeps others out.

        Can I try it without installing Discord?

        Yes. Hit "Play online" to open play.rambooo.com. No sign-in: you sit at a table with AI bots, and the AI Storyteller takes you from the first night to the end. The trial runs on its own server, nobody else can join, and there's no voice; your games don't count toward the leaderboard, but you can see the leaderboard across all servers. To play with friends over voice, set it up in Discord as below.

        How do I start a game?

        1. Follow the instructions in the GitHub repo (in Chinese) to invite the bot to your server (you need the Manage Server permission). 2. Everyone joins the same voice channel, the Host included: that's the Clock Tower Square. 3. In a text channel, click the app launcher next to the message box and pick "Blood on the Clocktower". Launching from a text channel means the bot moving people between voice channels won't kick you out of the Activity. 4. The Host picks a script, a Storyteller mode and house rules, and everyone clicks "Join game". Inviting the bot takes about a minute the first time; a game takes about an hour.

        Is the AI Host a large language model like ChatGPT?

        No. The AI Host is an automatic Storyteller written to the official rules: it builds the setup, wakes players, resolves the night, invents false info and helps the side that's behind, logging every ruling in the journal revealed at the end. It doesn't chat with you.

        How many players do I need?

        5 to 15. Short on players? The Host can add bot players in the lobby to fill the table.

        I've never played. Can I keep up?

        There's an intro animation and a practice game, with step-by-step tips and little demos: revealing your character, choosing at night, entering a house and locking the door, nominating and voting are all shown first. Hover over a rules term in an ability to see what it means.

        Does it work on mobile?

        Yes. The Discord mobile app can open the Activity too, and phones get their own portrait layout: the square becomes an upright circle, with panels along the bottom.

        Is it free? Is it official?

        Free. This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project and is not affiliated with The Pandemonium Institute.

        What if I disconnect or the server restarts?

        If you drop out, reopen the Activity and you're back in your seat. If the backend restarts, the game in progress is lost; deafened states are recorded and lifted automatically after the restart, so nobody stays muted.

        Can I host my own copy?

        Yes. All the code is in the GitHub repo; the README (in Chinese) explains how to create the Discord application, fill in .env and run it.
